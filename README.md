## How to Install the Extension

Chrome extensions in development are loaded as **unpacked extensions** through Developer Mode:

1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions/` by typing it into your address bar.
3. In the top-right corner of the page, toggle the **Developer mode** switch to **ON**.
4. Click the **Load unpacked** button in the top-left corner.
5. In the file picker dialog, select the workspace root folder:
   `/Users/narendrasisodiya/Github/chrome-script-executor`
6. The extension is now installed and will appear in your Chrome toolbar!

## How to Test and Run

1. Click the puzzle icon (or the extension icon) in the Chrome toolbar to open the **ScriptForge** popup.
2. Click **Launch Sandbox Testbed** at the bottom of the popup. This will open the built-in demo environment.
3. On the sandbox page:
   - Select a template (like the **Price Highlighter**).
   - Click **Share Script with Extension**.
   - A drawer will slide in from the bottom right. Click **Approve & Save**.
4. Open the extension drawer on the page by pressing **`Alt+Shift+S`** or clicking the floating **`⚡`** badge at the right edge of the screen.
5. Click **▶ Run** next to your script to execute it on the page and watch the target elements update instantly!
